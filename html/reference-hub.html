Three-step assessment approach

Adopted at the June 25, 2026 VA discussion.i

Screening: Vulnerability Rating = (Exposure + Sensitivity) × Criticality. Asset-level 0-3 scores, bucketed High / Medium / Low.
Open: how pair scores aggregate per asset. One asset appears in several pairs. A culvert carries FL-C, DF-C, and LS-C; a bridge carries FL-B, DF-B, LS-B, and AV-B. How those combine into a single asset-level rating is not decided: highest pair, sum, or weighted.i
Open: vendor risk scores versus this screening. Both contracted datasets ship their own pre-computed risk score, one an average-annual-loss measure for flood and one a net-value-change measure for wildfire. Each is structurally the same shape as the screening formula above. Recommendation: take the component layers as exposure and sensitivity inputs, score them through (E + S) × Criticality, and keep the vendor scores as validation cross-checks.i
Open: flood data licence expires July 2027. The contracted flood dataset is a one-year licence ending roughly two months after the final plan is delivered; the wildfire package is perpetual. Decide before wiring flood layers into the tool.i

Sensitivity indicators by asset

Roads MDOT

  • Pavement condition (all)
  • Pavement foundation / permeable sub-base (flooding)
  • Road elevation (flooding)
  • Depth to groundwateri
  • Asset age (all)

Bridges MDOT

  • Bridge condition, deck rating / type, material
  • Scour criticality (flooding, debris flow)
  • Bridge elevation (flooding, landslide, debris flow, avalanche)
  • Seismic retrofit (earthquake); channel & water-opening adequacy (flooding)

Culverts DCTC

  • Culvert condition (all)
  • Scour (flooding, debris flow, landslide, avalanche)
  • Culvert capacity (debris flow, landslide, avalanche)
  • Age; channel condition (flooding)

Active Transit SF Bay Trail

  • Paved vs. unpaved trail (all)
  • Elevation (flooding)
  • Trail slope (flooding)
