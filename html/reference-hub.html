Three-component assessment approach

Adopted at the June 25, 2026 vulnerability assessment discussion.i

Screening (Aug. 20, 2026 draft Vulnerability Assessment Methodology): Vulnerability = (Exposure Score x wE) + (Sensitivity Score x wS), with weights set per pair: wE 0.7 and wS 0.3 by default, 0.3 and 0.7 for bridges and culverts under flooding, and exposure only for transit centers, wildfire trails, and avalanche. Scores are asset-level, 0 to 3, and classed High, Medium, or Low. Criticality is assessed separately as component 1 and read with vulnerability to select scenario locations and TRIP priorities. The indicator rubrics per pair are on the Scoring Rubrics page.
Proposed: maximum pair score per asset. One asset appears in several pairs. A culvert carries FL-C, DF-C, and LS-C; a bridge carries FL-B, DF-B, and LS-B. TRPA proposes the asset-level rating as the maximum pair score (V_max, with V_max_pair recording the hazard). Summing would double-count one physical failure at one location. Lane default confirmed Oct. 8, 2026; consultant confirmation requested (decision 9 on the Scoring Rubrics page).i
Decided since June 25. Every flooding pair is scored in the vulnerability assessment; avalanche on bridges moved to TRIP discussion; marinas are retired as an asset class; landslide, avalanche, and debris flow are separate hazards throughout; and runout modeling is out of scope, so no runout model or terrain data is being purchased. Wind, winter storm, seiche, high lake level, and earthquake are tracked rather than dropped.i
Open: vendor risk scores versus this screening. The contracted wildfire package ships a pre-computed net-value-change risk score with the same structure as the screening equation above. Recommendation: the component layers are taken as exposure and sensitivity inputs, scored through the weighted exposure and sensitivity equation, and the vendor score is kept as a validation cross-check.i
Decided Oct. 8, 2026: no flood depth model. TRPA is not purchasing the commercial flood model the draft methodology assumed. Flood exposure is scored from FEMA flood zones and stream crossings for every horizon, and the culvert loading ratio carries the hydraulic and future-horizon signal.i

Sensitivity indicators by asset

Precedent indicators from the May 20, 2026 consultant deck; the indicators actually scored are on the Scoring Rubrics page.

Roads MDOT

  • Pavement condition (all)
  • Pavement foundation or permeable sub-base (flooding)
  • Road elevation (flooding)
  • Depth to groundwateri
  • Asset age (all)

Bridges MDOT

  • Bridge condition, deck rating and type, material
  • Scour criticality (flooding, debris flow)
  • Bridge elevation (flooding, landslide, debris flow)
  • Seismic retrofit (earthquake); channel and water-opening adequacy (flooding)

Culverts DCTC

  • Culvert condition (all)
  • Scour (flooding, debris flow, landslide)
  • Culvert capacity (debris flow, landslide)
  • Age; channel condition (flooding)

Active Transit SF Bay Trail

  • Paved versus unpaved trail (all)
  • Elevation (flooding)
  • Trail slope (flooding)
