Three-step assessment approach

The adopted approach (6/25 VA discussion, building on FHWA VAST guidance) runs three steps: a criticality assessment, a system-wide indicator-based screening, and scenario-based disruption analyses for the highest-risk hazards and locations. Network redundancy and adaptive-capacity questions are handled in the scenario step (RA2CE), not as a separate sub-index.

Screening: Vulnerability Rating = (Exposure + Sensitivity) × Criticality — asset-level 0-3 scores, bucketed High / Medium / Low

1. Criticality assessment

What are the critical assets in the region?

  • Scope: roads, bridges, trails, transit centers
  • Indicators: evacuation route, detour length, AADT, proximity to strategic assets, community priority zones
  • Live: Criticality Index app (18,253 segments)

2. System-wide screening

Where are the greatest vulnerabilities?

  • Scope: the 14 Include-in-VA pairs
  • Score exposure and sensitivity per asset, multiply by criticality
  • Output: High / Medium / Low vulnerability classes on an interactive map

3. Scenario-based disruption

How does the system react to an event? Where are the pinch points?

  • 2-3 plausible scenarios per high-priority hazard / location
  • RA2CE on the Overture network: detours, travel-time change, blocked assets, populations impacted
  • Optional monetization: Expected Annual Loss from loss functions and value of time

Sensitivity indicators by asset

Roads MDOT

  • Pavement condition (all)
  • Pavement foundation / permeable sub-base (flooding)
  • Road elevation (flooding)
  • Asset age (all)

Bridges MDOT

  • Bridge condition, deck rating / type, material
  • Scour criticality (flooding, debris flow)
  • Bridge elevation (flooding, landslide, debris flow, avalanche)
  • Seismic retrofit (earthquake); channel & water-opening adequacy (flooding)

Culverts DCTC

  • Culvert condition (all)
  • Scour (flooding, debris flow, landslide, avalanche)
  • Culvert capacity (debris flow, landslide, avalanche)
  • Age; channel condition (flooding)

Active Transit SF Bay Trail

  • Paved vs. unpaved trail (all)
  • Elevation (flooding)
  • Trail slope (flooding)

Assessment approaches

Qualitative screening

Qualitatively assess how an asset type may be impacted, driven by sensitivity.

Best for: initial screening to decide which pairs get detailed analysis.

Indicator-based

Asset-level scoring on a 0-3 or 0-5 scale, overlaying hazard data with asset locations to grade exposure.

Best for: physical-damage pairs.

Criticality-focused

Used where hazard exposure does not vary much geospatially or data is unavailable. Asks which assets are most critical and what happens if they fail.

Best for: operational-disruption pairs.

Disruption-risk tool: RA2CE selected

RA2CE (Deltares) is the selected disruption-risk engine, running on the Overture-derived street network. Volpe RDR was evaluated and not pursued. Analysis work lives in the repo's scripts/ra2ce folder; network criticality results are live in the Criticality Index app.

RA2CE Deltares · selected

  • Flexible, Python-based risk & resilience framework
  • Integrates hazard exposure, network criticality, economic impacts
  • Estimates direct damages + indirect disruption costs (detours)
  • Runs on the Overture street network built by the data team

Volpe RDR USDOT Volpe · not pursued

  • Tool suite for resilience investment and ROI analysis
  • Evaluated alongside RA2CE; capable but requires significantly more data, modeling, and implementation effort
  • Retained as a reference for BCA framing in Task 3.4
