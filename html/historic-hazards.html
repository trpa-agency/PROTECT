The observed hazard record for the Lake Tahoe Region: fire perimeters, mapped landslides, and earthquake epicenters against the TRPA boundary and member jurisdictions. Historical hazards are the ground truth the predictive exposure models are checked against.i The fire record is queried live from CAL FIRE and clipped to the TRPA boundary polygon; it is California only, so fires on the Nevada side are missing.i The landslide and earthquake layers cover both states.

Landslides

Sparse inside the Region: 2 deposits and 3 points. Mapped features, not dated events, so they do not respond to the year filter. The basin has effectively no systematic landslide inventory.
