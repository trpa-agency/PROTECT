  • Watershed. Drainage area to each culvert from the 2 m lidar DEM, with the road fill cut through every culvert so creeks pass where they should.
  • Flow. The 100-year storm from NOAA Atlas 14 and the land cover; basins over 1 sq mi use the USGS regional equations.
  • Capacity. What the pipe passes with water at the top of the opening. Real culverts take more, so the ratio errs high by 1.5 to 2.
  • Flags. A small recorded pipe on a big drainage is a record to check, not a score; it is withheld and listed for the owner. A ratio above 20 is listed too.
  • Flood exposure. The highest flood class anywhere in the culvert's drainage. The value at the pipe itself is in the table for comparison.

Draft v0.1. Culvert inventories from six jurisdictions and the TRPA compilation; bridges from the National Bridge Inventory; flood zones from FEMA. Method: docs/SCORING_RUBRICS.md 2.2 to 2.5. Internal review draft.
