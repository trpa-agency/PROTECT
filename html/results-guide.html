Draft for Workshop 1, Nov. 9, 2026 The Results page gives every road, culvert, bridge, path, and transit hub a score for each hazard. This page explains the score and the map in one sheet.

The scores are a draft. They rank assets and raise questions. They are not design values.

How an asset gets a score two steps, built on FHWA vulnerability assessment guidance

Step 1

Criticality: how much the asset matters

Traffic, the detour if the road closed, access to critical places, and who depends on it. Every road segment lands in a quarter, 0 (lowest) to 3 (top). Culverts and bridges take their road's quarter.

Step 2

Vulnerability: how likely the asset is to fail

Exposure (does the hazard reach the asset, and how hard) plus sensitivity (how badly it would fare), each scored 0 to 3 and weighted per hazard: Vulnerability = Exposure x wE + Sensitivity x wS. Roads are exposure only.

Class. Low under 1, Medium 1 to under 2, High 2 and over. Wildfire and debris flow start Medium at 1.5.
Highest hazard. An asset scored for several hazards takes its highest class. The popup names the hazard that set it.
Hotspot. A High-vulnerability road in the top criticality quarter.
Stand-in. A dataset filling in until the planned one arrives. Debris flow uses fire severity in place of soil burn severity.
Default. A sensitivity input with no record (most culvert conditions) takes the method's default. The popup says so.

Reading the map

Colors

  • Low Medium High not exposed or not scored (every tab).
  • Criticality quarter 0 to 3, dark to bright (Overview).
  • 100-year zone 500-year zone stream crossing (Flood exposure).
  • passes with room passes up to twice capacity two to four times over four times (Storm vs capacity). White: record to confirm. Gray: no check.

Lines and symbols

  • Roads solid, paths dashed, culverts circles, bridges squares, transit hubs small squares.
  • Overview: thicker lines are more critical roads. Hotspots are thick red lines.
  • Culverts and bridges appear once you zoom in, except on Flooding, where they always show.
  • Hazard layers (flood zones, burn probability, fire severity, susceptibility, avalanche zones) are switches, off by default.

Clicks and controls

  • Click any asset for its scores and to Add a workshop note.
  • Click a culvert to draw its drainage area: orange drains straight to it, blue dashed arrives through upstream culverts.
  • Wildfire: click a road or path to see the 300 ft buffer it was scored on.
  • Flooding: Design storm switches between the 100-year and 25-year storm. Filters narrow the map and table together. Every table has Export CSV.

Every field in the popups and tables is defined in the data dictionary. The full method is on the Scoring rubrics page, and step 1 on the Criticality page. All at trpa-agency.github.io/PROTECT/html/.
