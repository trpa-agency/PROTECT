      This page replaces the sticky notes in the Workshop 1 vulnerability assessment activity. For each hazard station, mark the assets and places you think are vulnerable, rate your concern, and say why. The data team combines everyone's notes for the report-out and the Tahoe Resilience Improvement Plan (TRIP).

      • Stations. The five hazards scored in the assessment: flooding, wildfire, landslide, debris flow, and avalanche. Picking a station turns on that hazard's draft exposure map; the layer list on the map lets you turn any layer on or off.
      • Assets. Roads (the 18,253-segment street network), the 40 National Bridge Inventory bridges, the compiled culverts with a flow profile, transit routes, and bike and pedestrian paths. If what concerns you is not on the map, drop a pin.
      • Concern. 1 is greatest concern, 3 is worth noting. Tick Missing from the draft results when the draft maps do not show something you know is vulnerable.
      • Sending. Send my notes posts to TRPA's ArcGIS Online with your name and agency; your email address is not sent, only a code made from it so your latest send replaces the earlier one. If sending fails, download the CSV of your notes and email it to the data team.
      • Report out. The Everyone and Report out tables combine sent and imported notes by asset, with the count of people who flagged it and their concern levels.

      Notes are kept in this browser until you clear them. Internal review draft; the workshop release is Nov. 9, 2026.
