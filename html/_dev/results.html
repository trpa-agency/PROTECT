How to use this page

  1. Pick a hazard from the tabs in the blue bar. Overview combines every hazard.
  2. Use the panel beside the map. The buttons at the top choose what the roads are colored by, with the legend under them. Switches turn layers on and off. Hover any small i for what a layer shows.
  3. Click anything on the map for its scores. Click a culvert to draw the land that drains to it.
  4. Make room for the map. Drag the panel's edge to resize it, or use the arrows in its blue header to fold it away. The full-screen button on the map hides everything else and floats the panel over the map.
  5. Scroll down for charts, a searchable table you can export to CSV, how the numbers are made, and key numbers.
  6. Leave a comment on any road, culvert, bridge, path, or hub: open its popup and choose Add a workshop note.
  7. Other pages are under Pages, at the right of the blue bar.
