PROTECT Risk Index and Resilience Tool

Task 3 · Resilience Improvement Plan
Open Risk Index Tool (MVP) → v0.5

The Lake Tahoe Basin's roads, culverts, and bridges face wildfire, flooding, landslides, avalanches, and intensifying storms. PROTECT Task 3 (FHWA grant) is building a risk-based vulnerability assessment and an interactive risk index mapping tool that scores every transportation asset against those hazards, feeding the 2027 Resilience Improvement Plan. The method follows the FHWA VAST framework, with Exposure, Sensitivity, Asset Value, and Adaptive Capacity combining with Criticality into one risk score per asset.

Source layers + pipelines (climate, storms, culverts, debris flow)→ Per-asset sub-indices (E, S, AV, AC)→ Master PROTECT index→ Risk Index Tool + Workshops→ 2027 Resilience Improvement Plan

How the pieces fit together

The Reference Hub records the framework decisions and tracks what data exists and what is missing; the pipelines turn raw sources into exposure inputs (future climate stressors, trigger-storm climatology, the culvert layer, modeled debris-flow hazard); and the Risk Index Tool is where assets, hazards, and scores meet the map. Meeting agendas and action items live in the repo docs folder, not on these pages.
