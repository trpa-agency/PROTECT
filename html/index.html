A vulnerability assessment and an interactive map tool for the Lake Tahoe Basin's transportation assets: which roads, bridges, culverts, active transportation routes, and transit centers are most vulnerable to flooding, landslide, wildfire, debris flow, and avalanche.i The results point the 2027 Tahoe Resilience Improvement Plan (TRIP) to the assets and places that need resilience investment first.
