Tahoe Regional Planning Agency

PROTECT Risk Index and Resilience Tool

Task 3 · Tahoe Resilience Improvement Plan
v0.8

Risk-based vulnerability assessment and interactive risk index mapping tool for Lake Tahoe Basin transportation assets.i Roads, bridges, and culverts are scored against flooding, landslide, wildfire, debris flow, and avalanche hazards; active transport and transit centers against flooding and wildfire. Results inform the priority assets and locations for resilience investment in the 2027 Tahoe Resilience Improvement Plan (TRIP).
