  • Likelihood (E1). Annual burn probability from the 2018 FSim outputs, classed on the draft's breaks: under 0.5 percent a year is 1, 0.5 to 1 percent is 2, over 1 percent is 3. Over 1 percent covers 6.9 km2 of the basin, so almost every scored segment is class 1 and the score spread comes from flame length.
  • Flame length (E2). Conditional flame length from the same outputs: under 4 ft is 1, 4 to 8 ft is 2, over 8 ft is 3.
  • Sampling. Each indicator is the highest value within 300 ft of the segment, path, or hub; the share of each segment's length in each likelihood class is in the table. The score is 0.7 x E1 + 0.3 x E2.
  • Zeros. FSim treats towns, water, and bare rock as non-burnable, so streets inside the urban cores score 0 for fire itself. Evacuation and smoke are criticality and component 3 questions, not exposure.
  • Vintage. The 2018 surfaces predate the 2021 Caldor Fire. The contracted fire-behavior package replaces them on the same breaks if it is delivered and reviewed in time for December.
  • Not yet scored. Road sensitivity (wooden guardrails) sits at the draft default of 1. Paths and hubs have no sensitivity term.

Draft v0.1. Fire inputs from the 2018 FSim outputs on TRPA storage; roads from the TRPA street network; paths and hubs from TRPA services. Method: docs/SCORING_RUBRICS.md 6.4, decision 22. Internal review draft.
