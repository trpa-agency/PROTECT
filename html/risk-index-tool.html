How the assessment is built

The assessment has three parts: which roads matter most, how vulnerable each asset is to each hazard, and what happens when a key road closes. The approach was adopted at the June 25, 2026 vulnerability assessment discussion and is detailed in the consultant's Aug. 20, 2026 draft methodology.i

Indicator screening: Vulnerability = (Exposure Score × wE) + (Sensitivity Score × wS)
  • Weights. Set per pair: 0.7 exposure and 0.3 sensitivity for most pairs; 0.3 and 0.7 for bridges and culverts under flooding; exposure only for transit centers, active transport under wildfire, and avalanche.
  • Scale. Asset-level scores on a 0 to 3 scale are classed High, Medium, and Low.
  • Criticality. Assessed separately in component 1 and read with vulnerability to select scenario locations and TRIP priorities.
  • On the map today. Streams_and_Flood_Zone, Avalanche_Zones, Fire, and the criticality network PROTECT_VA (Streets_Network_Tahoe).
  • Coming in v2. The v2 service adds bridges, culverts, active transport, and transit centers with exposure, sensitivity, criticality, and vulnerability per pair.

The scoring rules for each hazard and asset combination are on the Scoring Rubrics page, and the full methodology record is in the Reference Hub. Climate inputs are on the Climate Data page, and storm and debris-flow methods are on the Storm Events page.

About this tool

This tool maps the risk index across the Lake Tahoe Region's transportation network for technical partners. It is the Task 3.3 deliverable of TRPA's PROTECT grant.i

Built by the TRPA Science and Data and Transportation teams, with ICF as the climate resilience consultant.i
