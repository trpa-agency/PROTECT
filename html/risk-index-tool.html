Three-step assessment approach

The adopted approach (6/25 VA discussion, building on FHWA VAST guidance): (1) a criticality assessment - live in the Criticality Index app; (2) a system-wide indicator-based screening of the 14 Include-in-VA hazard-asset pairs; (3) scenario-based disruption analyses (RA2CE) for the highest-risk hazards and locations.

Screening: Vulnerability Rating = (Exposure + Sensitivity) × Criticality — asset-level 0-3 scores, bucketed High / Medium / Low

The full methodology record - sub-index definitions, weighting precedents from peer DOT plans, the sensitivity matrix, and the data and model inventory - lives in the Reference Hub. Climate inputs are documented on the Climate Data page; trigger-storm and debris-flow methods on the Storm Events page.

About this tool

The PROTECT Risk Index Tool is the Task 3.3 deliverable of TRPA's PROTECT Plan (FHWA Promoting Resilient Operations for Transformative, Efficient, and Cost-saving Transportation grant). It visualizes the FHWA VAST-based risk index across the Lake Tahoe Region's transportation network for technical partners.

Built by TRPA (Science & Data + Transportation) with ICF as climate-resilience consultant, on the standard TRPA dashboard stack (Calcite + ArcGIS Maps SDK). Live spatial data from maps.trpa.org. Interactive criticality scoring runs in the companion Criticality Index app.

Internal review draft. Not for public distribution. Phase 1 MVP; Workshop 1 release November 2026.
