About this page

This page shows what participants sent from the Vulnerability Assessment Feedback page at Workshop 1 on Nov. 9, 2026, for the data team and the Tahoe Resilience Improvement Plan (TRIP).

  • Cleaned records. When two people sent from the same browser, the later send repeated the earlier person's notes and flooded areas. Each repeat was removed and the copy from the person who wrote it kept. Test notes were removed too.
  • Concern. 1 is Greatest Concern, 2 Somewhat Concerned, 3 Just Adding a Note. Blank means the person did not pick one. The marker on the map shows the number.
  • Hazards. The hazards a person tagged on the note. A note with no hazard counts under Any hazard.
  • By asset. Notes grouped by the asset or pinned place, with how many people flagged it and their highest concern.
  • Tables. Each table filters with the panel and exports to CSV.

Data: data/processed/results/workshop_feedback.json, written by scripts/export_feedback_explorer.py from the reviewed feedback geodatabase. Internal review draft.
