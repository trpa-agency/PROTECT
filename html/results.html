How to use this page

  1. Pick a hazard from the tabs in the blue bar. Overview combines every hazard.
  2. Use the panel beside the map. The buttons at the top choose what the assets are colored by, with the legend under them. Switches turn layers on and off. Hover any small i for what a layer shows.
  3. Read the classes. Low is a score under 1, Medium 1 to under 2, High 2 and over; wildfire uses Low under 1.5, Medium 1.5 to 2, High over 2. Roads are solid lines, paths dashed, culverts dots, bridges squares, transit hubs small squares. Each hazard tab maps the assets that hazard scores.
  4. Click anything on the map for its scores, each with its class and the hazard that set it. Click a culvert to draw the land that drains to it; click anywhere else to clear it.
  5. Make room for the map. Drag the panel's edge to resize it, or use the arrows in its blue header to fold it away. The full-screen button on the map hides everything else and floats the panel over the map.
  6. Scroll down for charts, a searchable table you can export to CSV (click a row to find it on the map), how the numbers are made, and key numbers.
  7. Leave a comment on any road, culvert, bridge, path, or hub: open its popup and choose Add a workshop note.
  8. Take it with you. The two-page guide covers how the numbers were made and what every field means; print it or save it as a PDF.
  9. Other pages are under Pages, at the right of the blue bar.
